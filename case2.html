What to carry back to practice

  • UGT1A1 *28/*28 is a poor metabolizer: 70% irinotecan starting dose, titrated by neutrophil count.
  • DPWG advises no change for *1/*28 heterozygotes, unlike DPYD, where heterozygotes do need a reduction.
  • The germline result can steer the chemotherapy backbone, not just the dose.
  • MSS answers the immunotherapy question; the germline answers the dosing question.
  • The same variant does not mean the same drug. BRAF V600E is paired with anti-EGFR therapy in colorectal cancer, not with a MEK inhibitor as in melanoma.
Verify all dosing against the current CPIC guideline and FDA labeling at the time of use.
