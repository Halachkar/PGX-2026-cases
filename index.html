What to carry back to practice

  • Activity score 1.0 is partial DPD deficiency, not a contraindication: halve the start, then titrate.
  • The titration step is the one most often forgotten. Leaving a curative-intent patient at 50% risks undertreatment.
  • A normal panel does not rule out DPD deficiency. Monitor early cycles regardless.
  • Store the result as discrete, coded data so it fires an alert years from now.
